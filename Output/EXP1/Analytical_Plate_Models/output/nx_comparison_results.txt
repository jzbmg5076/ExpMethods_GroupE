INTERPRETATION
  Plate 1: The planar CG offset is about 0.0005 in. The normalized drawing-axis moments differ by at most 0.62%; after harmonizing thickness, all three differ by less than 0.05%.
  Plate 2: The roughly 0.215 in CG offset and 4-5% inertia differences mainly reflect the different NX opening geometry versus the simplified measured opening.
  Plate 3: In-plane CGs agree within about 0.001 in, and normalized inertias agree within about 0.02%.
  Plate 4: In-plane CGs agree within about 0.001 in. Normalized inertias differ by about 2.1%, consistent with the NX model retaining its exact hole/edge geometry and a 0.445-in thickness.
  Small residuals are expected because NX retains detailed hole cutouts and exact CAD profiles, while MATLAB intentionally uses simplified closed-form boundaries.
  NX callouts are rounded to four decimal places, so inferred masses and normalized values are approximate.
